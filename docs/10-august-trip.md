# 10 — The August trip: 10 days in Senegal (recon + seed mission)

Reality check: 10 days is not the 8-week pilot of doc 09 — company registration alone
takes a week and "first month free" trials need a month. So the trip has a different,
sharper goal:

**Leave Senegal with (1) live tracked vehicles still reporting to your dashboard after
you fly home, (2) one trained mechanic who can install without you, (3) 10+ owner
contacts with real willingness-to-pay evidence, (4) a coverage map you measured yourself,
and (5) the admin groundwork started.** The business list and full pilot come on trip #2.

## The ten days

| Day | Mission |
|---|---|
| 1 | Land. Orange SIM + Free SIM (passport). Data bundles. Evening: put an Orange SIM in one tracker at your lodging, confirm it reports to your dashboard — **Senegal is now live on your map.** |
| 2 | **Coverage drive:** tracker + phone in a taxi/thiak-thiak across Dakar (Plateau → Médina → Grand Dakar → Pikine → corniche). Repeat any dead segments with the Free SIM. You now have a measured coverage map. |
| 3 | **Mechanics:** Colobane/Petersen clusters, mid-morning, with the demo on your phone. Goal: one mechanic who says yes. Pay him to install box #1 on a borrowed/rented moto while you film the process. |
| 4 | **Seed installs:** 3–5 vehicles. Recruit via the mechanic + stand chiefs. Offer: free box + free tracking "as a test, I want your opinion" — recon trade, not a sale. Get each owner's WhatsApp. |
| 5 | Day trip (Thiès or Mbour, ~1 h): see the Jakarta moto-taxi economy first-hand, coverage-test on the road, talk to 2–3 moto-taxi owners at the garages. This is your future passenger-market intel. |
| 6 | **Owner demos:** 3–5 sit-downs via referrals. Show their city, live. Ask the money questions plainly: "Combien paieriez-vous par mois pour ça ?" — write every number down. Collect 10+ contacts. |
| 7 | **Demand-side intel:** talk to 5 businesses that dispatch thiak-thiaks daily (restaurants, pharmacies, boutiques). What do they pay per course? What goes wrong? Would they pay for tracked, accountable delivery? |
| 8 | Check the week of data from the seed vehicles. Interview their drivers (what annoyed them?). Re-tighten any loose installs. Refine the pitch based on what you heard on day 6. |
| 9 | **Admin day:** APIX (start SUARL registration — it can finish while you're gone with a local proxy/notary), lawyer meeting for the CDP filing quote, Orange Business + Wave Business: ask requirements, collect contacts. |
| 10 | Close the loop: leave every box installed and running (they are your Senegal beachhead), agree concrete next steps with the mechanic ("I'll send 20 boxes in October, same terms") and each seed owner. Update STATUS.md with everything. Fly home. |

## After you land back home

Your dashboard now shows real motos moving around Dakar every day, from your couch. That
is: your proof for investors/partners, your long-duration field test (heat, rain, network)
running unattended, and your warm re-entry — when you return with 20–50 boxes for the real
pilot (doc 09), the mechanic, the owners, and the paperwork are already waiting.

**Trip budget (flights excluded): ~$600–1,200** — SIMs and data ~$50, mechanic pay
~$30–50, seed boxes you already own, transport/day trips ~$150, APIX + notary start
~$100–200, lawyer consult ~$100–250, buffer.
